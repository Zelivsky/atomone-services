How Nakamoto Bonus Works

The Nakamoto Bonus is a unique reward mechanism in AtomOne that incentivizes decentralization.

Formula

issuance = supply × inflation

standard_reward = issuance × (1 − η) × stake / bonded
equal_bonus     = issuance × η / N

APR_validator = (standard_reward + equal_bonus) / stake
APR_delegator = APR_validator × (1 − commission)

Where:

  • η (eta) — Nakamoto Bonus coefficient (currently 0.17)
  • N — number of active validators (100)
  • x — validator's share of bonded stake
  • commission — fixed 5% for all AtomOne validators

Key Insight

Smaller validators receive a higher Nakamoto Multiplier because the equal bonus tranche (η) is divided equally among all validators, regardless of stake. This means delegating to smaller validators yields higher APR.

K_NB = (1 − η) + η / (N × x)

The multiplier is highest for validators with small stake share (x), making them more attractive for delegators seeking maximum returns.
